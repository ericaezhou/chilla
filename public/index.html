Questions

Am I paying today?

No. Signing up saves your spot. Before the pilot starts we'll email you to confirm the plan, and you can pass.

What does Chilla have access to?

Only the channels and meetings you add it to. It follows your workspace permissions, and you can remove it at any time.

Does my whole company need to install it?

No. One PM can add it to their workspace. Teammates just see a friendly bot when it nudges them.

Why chinchilla?

Chinchillas are soft and quick and they hear everything (look at the ears). Seemed right.
